# Student Social Connection App

## 1. Product Overview

Build a modern social connection application for **university and college students**.

The product is **NOT a traditional dating app**.

It is also NOT:

- Instagram
- Facebook
- WhatsApp
- Snapchat
- A university management portal
- A notes/lecture/schedule application
- A traditional profile-based social network

The core purpose is:

> **Help students discover what other students are doing and give them an easy, low-pressure way to join and meet people.**

The central idea is:

> **Meet people through activities, not through profiles.**

A user does not primarily open the app to browse people's photos or followers.

They open it to answer:

> **“What can I do right now, and who can I do it with?”**

---

# 2. Core Product Concept

The application is based around **live activities**.

Examples:

- Coffee
- Food
- Gaming
- Movie
- Walk
- Gym
- Football
- Study
- Explore
- Going out
- Talk

A student can create a live activity.

Example:

```text
☕ COFFEE

Canary Wharf

Trying a new coffee spot after class.

12 people joined

Starts in
00:34:18

[ Join ]
```

Another user sees the activity and can join it.

The important part:

## The application does NOT initially reveal who joined.

Do not show:

- Names of participants
- Profile photos of participants
- Gender
- Gender percentages
- Individual participant identities
- Follower counts
- Social popularity
- Other unnecessary personal statistics

Only show:

```text
12 people joined
```

This creates curiosity and removes some of the awkwardness of approaching strangers.

---

# 3. Product Philosophy

Traditional social applications are centered around:

```text
Profile → Browse → Follow → Message
```

This app should be centered around:

```text
Intent → Activity → Join → Connection
```

Example:

Instead of:

> “Hi, I am Ashish. Follow me.”

The interaction begins with:

> “I'm going for coffee at 7 PM.”

Someone else can simply join.

This creates a much more natural social interaction.

Every active activity should have a countdown.

Example:

```text

00:34:18
```

Do NOT rely solely on browser-local timers.

The client should synchronize with server time to prevent timer inconsistencies.

When countdown reaches:

```text
00:00:00
```

The activity changes status.

Possible statuses:

```text
UPCOMING
STARTING
LIVE
COMPLETED
CANCELLED
FULL
```
